About Me¶

I am an associate professor in School of Engineering, Institute of Science Tokyo (Science Tokyo). Currently, I'm interested in modeling techniques and algorithms on mixed-integer optimization and related problems. In particular, solving mixed-integer conic optimization problems is one of my ongoing research projects. I am also interested in applications of mathematical optimization to other fields, such as machine learning and so on.
